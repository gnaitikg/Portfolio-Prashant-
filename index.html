Digital Innovation in Fashion

Patagonia LoopID

Patagonia's Worn Wear resale program spent 15 to 20 minutes verifying and pricing each used item, while counterfeits flooded the market. We designed a Digital Product Passport: an NFC chip in every garment that shows where it was made, what it is made of, every repair and its resale value, stored on blockchain. The idea: stop selling products, start managing assets.

15 min to under 2Worn Wear processing time per item, with a chip that costs under $1 a unit
With Rakshith
Supply Chain Transformation

Oatly

Oatly sits between two supply chain models. We placed it on the maturity curve and wrote a five-year roadmap: protect the oat base IP, run a cloud ERP for real-time demand, and forecast from outside in.

5 yearsRoadmap, ending in modular plug-and-play manufacturing

Operations and Forecasting Risk

Sport Obermeyer

Over $5M of skiwear production was committed four months before retailers gave any data. We recommended delaying commitments until after the trade show, keeping fabric undyed longer, and sourcing risky styles for flexibility and safe ones for cost.

Data and Sports Analytics

Champions League goals

Which game statistics actually predict goals? We took 36 European teams from the Champions League and ran a backward-elimination regression. Only two variables survived: attempts on target and possession. The model explains over 70% of goals scored.

R² 0.72Share of goals explained by shots on target and possession, 36 teams

I build my own tools.

Friday is the personal operating system I built for myself. It started as a job search tool and grew into an app that runs my day: it finds and scores roles, plans my time, reads and drafts my email, preps me for case interviews, and answers by voice. It does nothing outward on its own. Every email or action waits for my approval.

It is not one dashboard. It is a set of connected systems, and the same pattern works for a business: pull data from the tools a team already uses, add rules and AI, and put a human approval in front of anything that matters. This is how it looks today.
