Sport Obermeyer: Demand Uncertainty and Sourcing Strategy

Over $5M of skiwear production was committed four months before retailers gave any data. We recommended delaying commitments until after the trade show, keeping fabric undyed longer, and sourcing risky styles for flexibility and safe ones for cost.

$5M+Committed before any real retailer data

The full story

Obermeyer had a genuinely tough problem. It was committing to over USD 5M in production decisions four months before it had any real retailer data, in fashion skiwear, where styles, colours and sizes multiply the uncertainty. Get it wrong and you either sit on unsold inventory or turn away customers who want your best sellers.

We diagnosed the root causes: extreme demand uncertainty, lead times of 45 to 90 days depending on the component, and a sourcing strategy not matched to product risk.

The fix was a combination. Delay production commitments until after the Las Vegas trade show, where early retailer signals emerge. Use fabric postponement to keep greige fabric undyed until demand is clearer. Segment sourcing so uncertain styles go to Hong Kong for flexibility while predictable classics go to China for cost. We also flagged that high disagreement within the buying committee is a useful risk signal, not noise.

A classic case, with good lessons on how uncertainty should drive decisions.

With Dayan, Maria Camila and 2 others. Prepared as part of the International MBA at IE Business School, Madrid.
